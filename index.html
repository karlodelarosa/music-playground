Circle of Fifths

Click any key — details appear on the right. Use the piano toggle if you want the keyboard.

How to use

  • Tap a slice on the wheel to select a key and hear its root.
  • Clockwise — each step is a perfect fifth (C → G → D → A → E…).
  • Counter-clockwise adds flats; clockwise adds sharps.
  • Neighbors share many notes — smooth key changes.
  • Right panel — signature, relative minor, scale, and diatonic chords.
